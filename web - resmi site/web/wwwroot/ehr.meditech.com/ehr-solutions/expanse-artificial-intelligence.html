Artificial Intelligence

The intelligent application of AI

At MEDITECH, the strategic use of artificial intelligence is central to our intelligent EHR platform. But we believe that AI should support the people making care decisions — not take away their control. We harness new technology to do the things humans can’t do: process massive volumes of data in milliseconds. And allow humans to do what only they should do: make care decisions. With Expanse, you’re always in control.

Reducing Burden

Supporting physicians, nurses, and patients

with intelligent AI solutions

  • Ambient Listening

    • Capture conversation between patient and provider
    • Automatically generate visit notes for review and editing by clinicians
    • Surface notes directly in the Expanse chart

  • Intelligent Search

    (Expanse Navigator)

    • Search across all data in the EHR
    • Structured and unstructured data, scanned documents, handwritten notes, faxes, legacy data, and more
    • Synthesize and rank results intuitively to aid clinical decision-making

  • Nursing Handoffs

    • Automatically generate handoff documents by extracting key details from patient records
    • Improve handoff communication by structuring and formatting information consistently
    • Surface key details that support patient safety

  • Hospital Course Summary

    • Automatically generate summary by extracting key details from patient records
    • Reduce variability between providers and improve clarity for post-discharge care teams
    • Save hours of manual work reviewing and summarizing complex stays

  • Automated Patient Communications

    (Expanse Patient Connect)

    • Automated, bi-directional SMS communication
    • Natural Language Understanding built into workflow that suggests responses to patient actions
    • Sentiment analysis to interpret intent — ensuring that messages are directed to the right staff and minimizing delays

  • Appointment No-Show Predictions

    • Assess the likelihood of patient no-shows using Machine Learning Pattern Analysis
    • Draw upon all available data, including past attendance history, appointment type, time of day, and SDOH to improve accuracy
    • Use predictions to optimize schedules, improve staff efficiency, and prioritize outreach
