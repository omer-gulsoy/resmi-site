Digital freedom starts with Expanse

Healthcare organizations want to feel empowered by their technology and an authentic EHR partnership that encourages independence. MEDITECH Expanse unites the continuum of care by catering to all specialties, with a concentration on mobility, intuitive functionality, flexibility, and user satisfaction. Expanse evolves with the healthcare industry, enabling organizations of all types and sizes to adapt in an ever-changing environment while experiencing the benefits of an intelligent EHR.

Everything you need on one platform

The Expanse platform is intelligent, interoperable, and integrated. Specialties achieve more on a digital ecosystem reinforced with intuitive, built-in features that enhance every aspect of care.

  • Population Health: Proactively manage your patient populations to streamline care and keep patients moving in the right direction with the Expanse Population Health Management solutions.
  • Precision Medicine: Leverage our Genomics solution in conjunction with Oncology, Pharmacy, and Pathology to build an effective precision medicine strategy, eliminating time-consuming trial and error by directing clinicians to the precise therapy or clinical trial.
  • Predictive Surveillance: Enable Expanse’s predictive surveillance solution to identify at-risk patients as it analyzes your data in the background, poised to alert care teams in real time if a patient’s status changes.
  • Clinical Decision Support: Empower your clinicians with clinical decision support that enhances their decision-making without disrupting workflows, improving patient safety and quality reporting goals.
  • Analytics: Visualize and capture data on Business and Clinical Analytics’ interactive dashboards to corroborate operational, financial, and clinical efforts.
