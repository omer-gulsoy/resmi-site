MEDITECH AS A SERVICE

The performance of Expanse, the power of a subscription

MEDITECH as a Service (MaaS) delivers the full Expanse intelligent EHR platform while reducing the time and cost of procuring, hosting, and maintaining an enterprise-level EHR.

With a wide range of functionalities that connect every setting, MaaS enables organizations to maintain autonomy, improve financial performance, and focus on providing care to patients.

Answering the needs of today’s health systems

MEDITECH’s Expanse platform helps address the increased pressures on health systems to meet value-based care targets, navigate constantly changing staffing levels, and deliver convenient, user-friendly tools for patients.

For providers:

  • Clinicians get a holistic view of their patients' medical histories, no matter when or where they have received care, with the Traverse Exchange network.
  • Expanse Surveillance identifies patients who need immediate attention and enables providers to take action.
  • With a library of intuitive widgets and shortcuts, customizable order sets, and streamlined common tasks, clinicians can tailor their user experience for their own to their personal workflows.

For patients:

  • Expanse helps patients communicate with their care team (even at a distance or between visits) with Expanse Patient Connect, Expanse Virtual Care, and MEDITECH’s MyHealthHub.
  • MEDITECH’s Revenue Cycle solution provides innovative, efficient tools that contribute to a positive consumer experience and connect every point of patient care.

For organizations:

  • Organizations of any size can extend and enhance their Expanse EHR with cloud hosting that includes multiple specialties, connects remote sites, and protects patient data.
  • MEDITECH Alliance is an ecosystem of vetted, leading-edge organizations who develop solutions that complement the capabilities of Expanse.
  • Authorized developers test their apps' integration with Expanse in the Greenfield Workspace testing environment to create new solutions that extend access to clinicians and patients.

Get more with MEDITECH and Google

Through our longtime collaboration with Google, MEDITECH designs, develops, and deploys cloud-based solutions available through MaaS:

  • Google Cloud delivers secure data storage and access with advanced cybersecurity measures to identify and mitigate potential threats.
  • Expanse Navigator harnesses the power of Google search and AI to analyze patient medical history and intelligently organize relevant data within the chart, saving time and reducing cognitive burden on clinicians.
  • MEDITECH offers a suite of solutions that improve clinician mobility, provider-patient communications, and organizational efficiency.
