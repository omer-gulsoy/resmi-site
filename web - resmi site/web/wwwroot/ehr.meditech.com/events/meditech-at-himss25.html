MEDITECH at HIMSS25

Embracing the future of health IT.

Healthcare organizations today need reliable, secure, and affordable technology for their clinicians and patients. MEDITECH Expanse offers a 360-degree solution that drives transformation.

Join us in booth #972 at #HIMSS25 to discover how you can realize your vision for the future.

Share in the power of data.

We know that healthcare organizations have different needs, budgets, and long-term goals. MEDITECH Expanse is an open and scalable platform designed to suit your needs, helping you stay competitive and maintain your independence.

Join us for our Lunch & Learn sessions on Tuesday and Wednesday, where you can enjoy a complimentary catered lunch generously provided by our sponsors while engaging with expert speakers.

Patient-Driven Healthcare: How EHR Technology is Fueling the Precision Medicine Revolution

Tuesday, March 4 | 11:30 a.m.

Discover how advancements in genomics, oncology, behavioral health, and pharmacy integration are revolutionizing patient care.

Moderator:

  • Jennifer Ford, Manager, Product Management, MEDITECH

  • Patricia Rice, MSN, ANP-BC, AGN-BC, OCN, Frederick Health
  • Sanaz Riahi, RN, PhD, Vice President, Clinical, Practice, and Chief Nursing Executive, Ontario Shores Centre for Mental Health Sciences

Beyond the Bedside: Intelligent Tools for Today’s Nurses

  • Cathy Turner, BSN, MBA, RN-BC Chief Marketing and Nurse Executive, MEDITECH

Panelists:

  • Helen Wong, RN, BSN, Clinical Informatics Supervisor, Emanate Health
  • Kelsey Reed, DNP, MHA, MSN, FNP-C, Director, Patient Care, Phoebe Putney Health System

From Data to Decisions: Advancing Healthcare with Smart Analytics and Intelligent Interoperability

Tuesday, March 4 | 12:15 p.m.

Learn how MEDITECH’s Traverse Exchange network is leading the way in intelligent interoperability by helping organizations maintain their independence. Attendees will also discover how advanced analytics are elevating healthcare by improving outcomes locally and globally.

Transforming the Provider Experience: Smart Tools for Smarter Healthcare Delivery

Wednesday, March 5 | 12:15 p.m.

Explore the Intelligent EHR’s transformative potential in alleviating cognitive load and documentation burdens.

Moderator:

  • Rebecca Lancaster, Director, Product Management, MEDITECH

Panelist:

  • Kristin Conley, DO, Chief Medical Information Officer, Frederick Health

“It is imperative that we unburden clinicians so they can focus on what matters most — the relationships with their patients. MEDITECH's focus is on transforming the clinician experience. We're solving real challenges today by supporting personalization and mobility; tackling the documentation burden through the infusion of ambient listening, conversational and generative AI; and more efficiently leveraging data through intelligent search and summarization capabilities.”

Helen Waters

MEDITECH Executive Vice President and COO

Stand together, remain independent.

From executives navigating the complexities of modern healthcare to clinicians leveraging Expanse to drive patient-centered care, join your peers as they share their innovative strategies for success.

Here are some of your customer peers who will be sharing their success during General Education sessions at HIMSS25.

Innovative Smart Hospital Design: Harmonizing Patient Care and Operational Excellence

Tuesday, March 4 | 10:15 a.m.
Caesars, Forum 116

Eric Carey

Vice President and CIO, Valley Health System

Andrea Valtos

Assistant Vice President, Valley Health System

Kathleen McDonough

Director of Operations and Logistics, The Valley Hospital

Valley Health System opened its smart hospital on April 14, 2024, featuring advanced technology to enhance care. This session shares insights from leaders on design and implementation, offering practical advice for smart hospital projects.

Expanding Use Cases for Genomics: A Precision Medicine Update

Tuesday, March 4 | 3:15 p.m.
Venetian, Lido 3101

Patricia Rice

Clinical Director, Precision Medicine and Genetics, Frederick Health

Jackie Rice

Vice President and CIO, Frederick Health

Frederick Health is pioneering physician access to ordering, storing, and displaying patient genetic data. Its Precision Medicine program has achieved over 50% efficiency gains, encouraging physicians to integrate genetic insights into practice. Join Jackie and Patricia as they discuss these advancements, the benefits of genomics for clinicians, and the program's expansion into precision nutrition, behavioral health, and clinical trial matching.

Building Healthier Communities: Strategies for Prioritizing Equitable, Culturally Competent Care

Wednesday, March 5 | 3:15 p.m.
Venetian, Veronese 2501

Daisy Rodriguez, MSN, MPH, BS, RN

Chief Operating Officer, Humboldt Park Health

Addressing healthcare disparities and social determinants of health is essential for equity. By reducing these disparities, we enhance individual and community health. Join Daisy Rodriguez as she shares Humboldt's strategies for supporting diverse patients and emphasizes the role of data in improving patient quality of life and health.

Defragmenting Your Analytics Model Through a Centralized Governance Approach

Thursday, March 6 | 12:45 p.m.
Venetian, Palazzo M

Mark Galea

Senior Vice President and CIO, Willis-Knighton Health System

Mark Galea explains how Willis-Knighton Health System transformed its fragmented analytics into a structured, transparent strategy focused on data governance, management, and analytics. Learn about their data governance board, request prioritization process, consistent calculation methods, and the results, including a 50% reduction in reporting resources.

Education Theater Sessions

Transforming Healthcare Through Ambient Listening and Generative AI: From Clinical Documentation to Comprehensive Care Intelligence

Tuesday, March 4 | 1:00 p.m.
Smart Theater

Moderator:

  • Mitchell Josephson, CEO, Health Data Management Group

Panelists:

  • Rebecca Lancaster, Director, Product Management, MEDITECH
  • Kenn Harper, General Manager, Dragon Product Management, Microsoft
  • Cate Buley, MD, FAAFP, FACHE, Chief Medical Officer, SouthEast Alaska Regional Health Consortium (SEARHC)

This session delves into the revolutionary impact of ambient listening and generative AI on healthcare, transcending documentation and examining the broader impact on clinical efficiency and operations. This discussion will also address respective responsible AI development philosophies, the importance of equitable access, and practical implementation strategies for diverse care settings.

Navigating the Subtleties of Generative AI in Healthcare: Deployment, Trust, and Responsible Innovation

Wednesday, March 5 | 11:15 a.m.
Smart Theater

  • Dr. Peter Clardy, Senior Clinical Specialist, Google

Rooted in responsible AI principles, the conversation will explore the trajectory of AI’s impact on care delivery.
